Privacy Policy (summary)

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address, name (if provided) and account identifier when you sign in with Apple, Google or email
  • Profile: username, display name, icon, bio and coffee experience
  • Gear you use, and what you post: recipes, brew logs, comments, photos and videos
  • Coffee beans: details of the beans you have at home (including purchase price, where you bought them and photos of the bag) and "My own beans"
  • Activity: likes, saves, follows, blocks, reports and use of the share button
  • A device token for push notifications, if you allow notifications
  • Access logs: IP address, date and time, device and OS type
  • Advertising and device information sent to Meta for measuring our ads, and the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking on your iPhone
  • Pourly Plus purchase records: purchases, renewals and cancellations, use of the free trial, and transaction identifiers
  • Survey answers, and contact details you choose to give us for a thank-you gift

We do not collect your location, your contacts or your payment details. Apple handles all payments.

How we use it

To provide, maintain and improve Pourly; to identify you and sign you in; to send notifications when someone brews or comments on your recipe or follows you; to show statistics such as average satisfaction for each recipe; to handle reports and breaches of the Terms; to investigate faults and prevent misuse; to answer enquiries; to run surveys and send thank-you gifts; to send news about new features and campaigns (only if you turn this on, and you can stop at any time); to measure and improve our ads on Meta services; and to provide Pourly Plus, check your subscription status, restore purchases and answer related enquiries.

What is public

Your profile, follows and follower counts, public recipes, brew logs, comments, and attached photos and videos. Brew logs include the bean name and the number of days since roasting. "My own beans" are never shown to other people.

What is not public

Your email address, the price, shop and photos of your beans at home, "My own beans", your purchase records, your saved recipes, your blocks and reports, and private recipes. Photos and videos in private recipes are stored at hard-to-guess addresses and can be seen by anyone who has the address.

Sharing and service providers

We do not give your information to third parties without your consent, except where required by law, to protect life, body or property when consent is hard to obtain, or to the service providers below:

  • Supabase, Inc. (USA): data storage (servers in Tokyo)
  • Apple Inc., Google LLC and Expo (650 Industries, Inc.): delivering push notifications
  • Google LLC (Gmail): sign-in code emails and enquiry emails
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (USA): forwarding enquiry emails
  • Resend, Inc. (USA): report notification emails
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (USA): managing Pourly Plus purchase records (your account identifier and purchase records only; not your name, email or payment details)
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (USA): measuring our ads (device identifiers, device information, app activity, and the IDFA only if you allow tracking; not your name, email, posts, purchase records or payment details)

Information sent from your device to other companies (external transmission)

Under Japan's Telecommunications Business Act, we publish what the app sends directly from your device to other companies. The Japanese page is the official version: https://pourly.asthiro.com/external.html

  • RevenueCat, Inc. (from version 1.2): Pourly Plus purchase records, account identifier, device information (OS version, model, language), store country and IP address. Purpose: checking your subscription status and restoring purchases
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (Meta SDK): an identifier Meta assigns to your device, device information, app activity such as launches, and IP address; the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking. Purchase records are not sent. Purpose: measuring and improving our ads on Instagram and other Meta services. Every feature works whether or not you allow tracking; you can change this in your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
  • Supabase, Inc.: sign-in information, what you enter and post, your activity such as likes, saves and follows, IP address, and device and OS type. Purpose: storing and providing your data (the service itself)
  • Google LLC: information needed to sign in with Google (only if you choose Google)
  • Apple Inc.: information needed to sign in with Apple (only if you choose Apple), and device registration for push notifications if you allow notifications
  • 650 Industries, Inc. (Expo): your push notification token and app identifier, if you allow notifications. Purpose: delivering push notifications

Ads

If you do not use Pourly Plus, Pourly shows ads for products such as coffee equipment (affiliate links, including Amazon). We may receive a commission if you buy through these links. We do not record whether you tap an ad. When you open a link, the retailer's own privacy policy applies.

Keeping and deleting your information

When you delete your account, we delete your personal information and posts within a reasonable period, including "My own beans", the beans you have at home with their photos, and your purchase records (including those held by RevenueCat). Shared bean information you added to "Community beans" remains, but it no longer identifies you. Records held by Apple follow Apple's rules. If you cannot open the app, you can request deletion by email: https://pourly.asthiro.com/delete-account.html

Your rights

You can ask us to disclose, correct, stop using or delete your information. Contact us at support@asthiro.com and we will respond within a reasonable period after confirming your identity.
